## BEGIN PROMPT — paste everything between markers

Design a single static cover image for Vouch at 1920×1080 (16:9). One frame, no motion. Apply the Vouch Design System throughout — cream surface, indigo accents, Fraunces + Inter + JetBrains Mono typography. Choose your own composition, hierarchy, and visual treatment within the brand constraints below.

## What Vouch is (so the image earns its keep)

Vouch is voice-recorded payment protection for peer-to-peer marketplace sales. The AI mediator (Vera) captures the agreement between buyer and seller by voice, then Stripe holds the buyer's money safely until the item arrives. Built on Stripe Connect + ElevenLabs ConvAI.

The cover image needs to communicate **"voice-mediated payments you can trust"** in one glance.

## Hard constraints

- Cream `#f6f5f2` is the base canvas. May incorporate dark `#0c0c14` panel/strip/half-bleed if the composition calls for it.
- Pages and visual elements render flat — no 3D perspective tilts.
- Solid indigo italic Fraunces (`#5266eb`) for one emphasised word per headline. No gradients.
- No "escrow" in any visible copy. Use "money held" or "locked safely" instead.
- Brand voice: warm-editorial, anti-decoration. No confetti, no sparkle, no emoji ornaments, no generic fintech sky-blue.
- Tone: confident, restrained, editorial. NOT product-launch hype.

## Must include

- **Vouch wordmark** — Fraunces 600 weight, indigo dot logo, hero-scale
- **One primary tagline** — pick the strongest:
  - *"Receive your money, on time."* (with solid indigo italic on "*time.*")
  - *"Every deal, kept."* (with solid indigo italic on "*kept.*")
  - *"Trust the handshake. Hold the money."* (alternative if Claude prefers)
- **Vera waveform signature** — the 3-bar indigo waveform somewhere subtle (small)
- **Tech-stack badge** in mono small caps, 0.18em tracking, muted: *"Built on Stripe Connect + ElevenLabs ConvAI"*

## Optional additional elements (Claude's call)

- Subtitle line in mono / smaller Fraunces if the composition needs more context
- A status pill (e.g. MONEY HELD pulsing) anchored somewhere as a product hint
- A subtle reference to the moat — e.g. small waveform with one bar highlighted, or a hint at "voice on the record"
- A buyer/seller name pairing (Sarah → Marcus) if it adds narrative
- The cracked-iPhone photograph as a small inset — only if you want to imply "and when things go wrong, we handle that too"

## Mood direction

Think: **Stripe annual report cover** × **NYT editorial photo essay** × **Mercury bank product page**. Confident, warm, anti-decoration. Reading the cover should feel like opening a well-designed magazine, not a SaaS landing page.

## Composition discipline

- Use whitespace generously — the cream surface is the brand
- One focal point that draws the eye first (wordmark OR tagline, not both fighting)
- Tech-stack badge sits humbly — not a brag, just a credential
- Avoid centred-symmetric "balance" — a thoughtful asymmetric layout reads more editorial

## What to avoid

- Gradient text effects (use solid colours)
- Hand-drawn elements, sparkles, scribbles, marker effects
- Iconography clusters (Stripe + ElevenLabs logos as colourful badges)
- Stock-photo people smiling at phones
- 3D perspective on any element
- The word "escrow" anywhere
- Multiple status indicators stacked decoratively
- A literal "trust handshake" illustration — too obvious

## Deliverable

A single PNG, 1920×1080 (or 16:9 ratio at any resolution). Static. No animation needed — this is a poster/cover, not a film frame.

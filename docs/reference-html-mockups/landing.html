Three steps · 60 seconds

From handshake to deposit, recorded.

Vouch combines what banks have done for centuries — holding money in escrow — with what voice can do now: make agreements clear, recorded, and enforceable.

01 · Voice

Speak the terms.

Both parties have a short voice call with Vera, your AI mediator. She asks the right questions, catches the ambiguities, reads the contract back. Both say "I agree" — and the agreement is on the record.

02 · Stripe

Money locks in.

The moment both sides voice-agree, Stripe holds the funds. Neither party can touch it until the deal completes — or Vera mediates a dispute. Real money, real protection.

03 · Deliver

Confirm by voice.

When the item arrives or the work is delivered, a 10-second voice check releases the money. If something's wrong, Vera mediates — with the original recording as evidence.
